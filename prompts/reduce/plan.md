## The member's plan
The order states the member's plan. A plan named without FEP, SAO or another program (for example a PPO or HMO product) means the member is not in that program: leave out a condition or route only for that program. A condition that only asks which plan, program or line of business the member
belongs to (for example FEP, SAO, Medicare Advantage, a specific product) is decided by the order: if the order's plan
satisfies it, leave it out; if the order's plan makes a route impossible (for example a route only for FEP members),
leave that route out. Say what you pruned in notes. Contract or benefit wording ("subject to the member's benefit
terms", "per the member contract") is not a medical-necessity condition: put it in notes, not in the tree.
