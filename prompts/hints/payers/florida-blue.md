Florida Blue guideline. Sections in fixed order:
- POSITION STATEMENT: the coverage logic. Conditions live here.
- DESCRIPTION, DEFINITIONS, RELATED GUIDELINES, REFERENCES, COMMITTEE/UPDATE history: admin. Exception: a definition the criteria rely on (a threshold, what a required step consists of, a term the indication names) is a condition.
- BILLING/CODING: code lists, admin. A code tagged (Investigational)/(Non-covered) is an exclusion.
- REIMBURSEMENT: read every line. Limits, combination denials, device-replacement rules and required documentation contents are conditions; payment mechanics (rental, purchase, stop billing, records on request) are admin.
- PROGRAM EXCEPTIONS: FEP/SAO/Medicare lines are plan, not chart. The step-therapy exemption paragraph is a route.
Scope words (FDA-approved, age, side/site, device type) stay conditions wherever printed.
