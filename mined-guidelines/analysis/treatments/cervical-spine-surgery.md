# Cervical spine surgery

Documents profiled (in `sourcing/analysis/profiles/`): Florida Blue 02-20000-45 (anchor), BCBS Illinois SUR712.028, BCBS Massachusetts 585, Carelon MSK03-1024.2 (vendor guideline, spine surgery module). Scope differs: Florida Blue and Carelon cover fusion, discectomy, laminectomy and disc arthroplasty; BCBSIL and BCBSMA cover only cervical disc arthroplasty (CDA). Blank "n/a" cells below mean the document does not rule on that procedure, not that the payer permits it.

## What it is

Cervical spine surgery treats nerve-root or spinal-cord compression, instability or deformity in the neck: discectomy (remove a disc), fusion (join vertebrae, usually with ACDF), laminectomy or laminoplasty (decompress the cord from behind), and disc arthroplasty (replace the disc with a motion-preserving device instead of fusing). Payers gate it on: a defined diagnosis (radiculopathy, myelopathy, instability, deformity, stenosis), matching symptoms or neurologic signs, imaging that shows compression at the symptomatic level, a failed course of conservative care (usually 6 weeks, with a bypass for progressive deficit), and, for arthroplasty, a device-and-anatomy screen (FDA-approved device, C3-C7, one or two levels, no list of contraindications such as prior fusion, instability or bone disease).

## Agreement and disagreement

Where they align
- CDA core: FDA-approved device, skeletally mature, C3-C7, at most 2 contiguous levels, 6 weeks conservative care, imaging proof, no prior fusion at another level, no active infection, no malignancy. Florida Blue, BCBSIL and BCBSMA are near copies (all derive from BCBSA 7.01.108); BCBSIL and BCBSMA text matches Florida Blue's CDA section almost word for word, including the "fully healed" requirement for staged second levels.
- Disc plus fusion hybrids and 3+ levels are rejected by all four.
- Every payer has a short-course conservative therapy default of 6 weeks.

Where Florida Blue is STRICTER
- CDA symptom bar: Florida Blue's list of at least one of seven findings (fine-motor loss, hyperreflexia and bilateral Babinski, tandem walk failure, etc.) is a myelopathy-type sign list attached to "radicular pain or myelopathy". On its face a patient with purely radicular pain and a nerve-root deficit (e.g. biceps weakness) matches none. BCBSIL needs only "functional and/or neurological deficit"; BCBSMA and Carelon accept objective neuro findings or pain.
- CDA has no rapid-progression bypass in Florida Blue; BCBSIL, BCBSMA and Carelon waive the 6 weeks for severe/progressive compression. (Florida Blue does have a bypass in discectomy and laminectomy: "Rapidly progressing neurologic deficits".)
- Bone disease: Florida Blue (and IL/MA) exclude any "osteopenia"; Carelon excludes only osteoporosis by T-score <= -2.5. Florida Blue also excludes RA/autoimmune disease and any "presence of facet arthritis"; Carelon excludes only "severe facet joint arthropathy" and does not list RA.
- Fusion for myelopathy: read literally, the flat list requires conservative therapy for 6 weeks AND injection failure even when the patient has myelopathic signs; Carelon waives conservative care for myelopathy and for progressive deficit and never asks about injections.
- Discectomy needs recent (6 months) MRI/CT-myelogram and daily VAS >= 4 on the pain pathway; Carelon sets no imaging age (except synovial cyst).
- Behavioral/addiction evaluation ("if any") is required in discectomy and laminectomy; no equivalent in Carelon (tobacco is only recommended).

Where Florida Blue is LOOSER
- Axial neck pain: Florida Blue allows fusion for non-radicular neck pain after 6 months of PT plus analgesics with 1-2 level disc pathology or instability; Carelon lists "Isolated neck pain ... without MRI evidence of intrinsic cord compression" as not medically necessary.
- Laminectomy for persistent pain (VAS >= 4) with stenosis; Carelon's laminoplasty route needs myelopathy and cord compression.
- Conservative therapy flexibility: fusion needs only 2 of 4 modalities (activity modification, exercise, analgesics, PT); Carelon needs PT (unless exception) plus a complementary modality for any conservative-management reliance. Florida Blue allows documentation "of why the candidate could not tolerate physical therapy"; BCBSIL/MA do not mention an exception.
- No kyphosis cap on laminectomy; Carelon caps kyphosis at 13 degrees for laminoplasty and lists focal kyphosis as a CDA contraindication, which Florida Blue does not.
- Florida Blue lists no explicit instability thresholds (mm, degrees) while others (BCBSMA 3.5 mm/11 degrees, Carelon 3 mm/11 degrees) do; this makes Florida Blue less measurable rather than looser or stricter.

Unique to one payer
- Florida Blue: laminectomy causes list (stenosis, OPLL, ligamentum flavum hypertrophy); VAS >= 4 definition; evaluation of cognitive/behavioral/addiction issues; failed epidural/facet injections as a fusion prerequisite.
- BCBSMA: prior-authorization form and fax numbers; investigational "neck or arm pain of unknown etiology", "absence of neck and/or arm pain", "progressive neurological deficit" (which conflicts with its own bypass 3b).
- BCBSIL: neck/arm pain AND deficit AND imaging as three required documents; x-rays accepted as imaging; lumbar disc criteria in the same document (ODI >= 40, 6 months).
- Carelon: CDA myelopathy pathway, failed-arthroplasty revision criteria, pseudoarthrosis, implant failure, synovial cyst, degenerative kyphosis, OPLL; independent-radiologist rule; the general definitions layer; peer-to-peer escalation.

## Structure

- Layout. Florida Blue is one Position Statement of four bulleted sub-policies. BCBSIL/BCBSMA are 1-3 pages of numbered criteria followed by 40+ pages (IL) or 3 pages (MA) of description and evidence. Carelon is a 66-page multi-topic manual: a general framework (pp.4-6), a definitions layer (pp.7-9, repeated at p.18), then indications.
- Logic shape. Florida Blue: pathways, each an all-of with mixed AND/OR bullet nesting up to depth 4; one k-of-n (2 of 4 modalities). BCBSIL/MA: all-of numbered list with one OR (3a/3b) and a NOT list ("investigational for all other indications"). Carelon: "medically necessary to treat ANY of the following conditions" (pathways) with a shared definitions layer that individual indications override ("The specific requirements take precedence over any stated general requirement").
- Where criteria live. Florida Blue: Position Statement only; the Description is background. BCBSIL: Coverage section only, with the real contraindication list placed in the investigational statement. Carelon: Clinical Indications + Contraindications + Exclusions + General Information, in four places per module.
- Delegation. Florida Blue to LCD L39799 (Medicare Advantage), FEP and SAO guidelines. Carelon to the health plan's own policy "in lieu of" Carelon if requested, and to CMS for Medicare Advantage. BCBSMA to BCBSA 7.01.108 and a separate lumbar policy. No MCG/InterQual reference in these four.
- Codes. Florida Blue lists CPT only, unmapped to criteria. BCBSMA ties codes to criteria (CPT and ICD-10-PCS "MUST be met for the following codes to be covered"). BCBSIL's disclaimer says presence or absence of codes is irrelevant. Carelon lists codes per module, "not meant to be all-inclusive".

## Problems for an automated checker

1. Florida Blue fusion block: grouping is implied by bullets only. "Cervical radiculopathy or myelopathy that included at least 1 of the following: ... Bowel and bladder incontinence, AND Pain that is unresponsive to at least 6 weeks ... AND Failure of epidural steroid injections or facet joint injections ... AND Imaging studies ... OR Treatment of axial neck pain ..." The AND/OR scope is a judgment call; a parser could attach the pain, injection and imaging requirements to every pathway.
2. "Cervical radiculopathy or myelopathy that included at least 1 of the following" (fusion, CDA): the sign list reads as myelopathy signs. A radiculopathy-only patient likely cannot satisfy it, but the text does not say so.
3. Open-ended modifiers a chart cannot answer: "Failure of epidural steroid injections or facet joint injections (if appropriate and not contraindicated)"; "Evaluation and appropriate management of associated cognitive, behavioral, or addiction issues, if any" (absent notes do not prove absence of issues); "prescription-strength analgesics for several weeks at a dose sufficient to induce a therapeutic response"; "Improvement in symptoms has failed or plateaued"; "Severe neck pain".
4. Level correlation: "at a level that corresponds with symptoms" requires reading the radiology report against the exam, a clinical judgment, not a fact.
5. Dates: discectomy imaging "within the past 6 months" (of what: request, service?). Carelon: clinical reevaluation "in reasonable proximity to the anticipated date of service".
6. Carelon tracked changes: the text has red-strikethrough/blue-underline marks flattened ("may be is performed", "indicating suggesting", "to should outweigh"); deleted and inserted words appear together. Pull text from the clean sentence by hand or via the PDF's colours.
7. Carelon radiculopathy: "Objective neurologic findings ..., progressive or severe neurologic deficits ..., and/or unremitting radicular pain which has not responded to at least 6 weeks of appropriate conservative management (physical therapy optional)" is one bullet in the CDA "ALL" list but an "ANY" list in decompression; the "(physical therapy optional)" collides with the general rule that conservative management "must include ... physical therapy".
8. Carelon "which may include" myelopathy sign lists: is any one sign required, and can an unlisted sign count?
9. Florida Blue update history describes criteria that no longer exist (nicotine cessation, home exercise program, "and/or clonus"); a reader or LLM can import them as current.
10. BCBSMA: "Progressive neurological deficit or deterioration" is listed as investigational yet 3b allows "severe or rapidly progressive symptoms ... requiring hospitalization"; the criteria contradict each other.
11. Criteria a chart rarely shows: skeletal maturity "by growth plate closure" (Carelon), "fully healed" initial implant, DEXA T-score for osteoporosis (Carelon), "prior surgery at the treated level" when operative notes are outside the record, independent radiologist read.
12. Contraindications for CDA in IL are the investigational list, not labelled contraindications; BCBSMA's list repeats entries (Paget's, metabolic bone disease).

## Test cases

1. Radiculopathy with a deficit but only 3 weeks of care. 48-year-old, right C6 radiculopathy, documented biceps weakness 4/5 and diminished biceps reflex, MRI C5-C6 herniation compressing the C6 root, 3 weeks of NSAIDs and PT, VAS 7, deficit stable. Carelon approves decompression: "Cervical radiculopathy ... imaging studies demonstrate nerve root compression ... and ANY of the following: Objective neurologic findings which correlate with a cervical nerve root impingement"; no conservative-care duration is required on that branch. Florida Blue denies discectomy: needs either "Rapidly progressing neurologic deficits" or deficit "refractory to at least 6 weeks of conservative nonsurgical therapy" with PT and analgesics; the deficit is stable and only 3 weeks passed.
2. Single-level arthroplasty with osteopenia. 41-year-old, C5-C6 herniated disc, neck/arm pain, myelopathic hyperreflexia with Babinski, 8 weeks of PT and medications, MRI confirms, FDA-approved disc, DEXA T-score -1.8 (osteopenia). Carelon approves (contraindication is "Osteoporosis defined as ... T-score of negative 2.5 or lower"). Florida Blue, BCBSIL and BCBSMA deny: "Metabolic bone disease (eg, osteoporosis, osteopenia, osteomalacia)" is a contraindication (Florida Blue: "Candidate is free from contraindications"; IL/MA: investigational).
3. Axial neck pain fusion. 52-year-old, nonradicular neck pain, MRI disc degeneration at C5-C6 and C6-C7 only, 7 months of PT and prescription NSAIDs with a plateau. Florida Blue approves fusion under "Treatment of axial neck pain when ... Pain is nonradicular ... Imaging demonstrates disc pathology ... limited to one or two levels ... failed or plateaued following at least 6 months". Carelon denies: "Isolated neck pain ... without MRI evidence of intrinsic cord compression" is listed under Exclusions as not medically necessary. (Arthroplasty is not in play: BCBSIL/MA have no fusion policy.)
