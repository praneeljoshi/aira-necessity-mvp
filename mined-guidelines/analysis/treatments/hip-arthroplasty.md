# Hip arthroplasty: cross-payer analysis

Documents profiled: Florida Blue 02-20000-50 (7 pp.), BCBS Rhode Island Total Joint Arthroplasty Hip and Knee (9 pp., hip + knee), Blue Shield of California BSC7.11 (19 pp.), Kaiser Permanente NCP 117 (8 pp.). Profiles are in `sourcing/analysis/profiles/`.

## What it is

Hip arthroplasty replaces the hip joint (ball and socket) with prosthetic components: total hip replacement (CPT 27130), conversion of earlier hip surgery (27132), revision of a prior replacement (27134, 27137, 27138), and, in some policies, resurfacing (S2118) or hemiarthroplasty. Payers gate it on a qualifying diagnosis (advanced arthritis, avascular necrosis, fracture, tumor, failed prior surgery), pain and functional loss, radiographic proof, a documented failed trial of non-surgical care, and increasingly on surgical-risk screens (BMI, diabetes control, tobacco use, infection).

## Agreement and disagreement

Agreement: all require arthritis/AVN/trauma or similar diagnosis, radiographic evidence, a failed non-surgical trial for arthritis pathways, and absence of active infection. Fracture, tumor and revision pathways in each skip or shrink the conservative-therapy requirement.

Disagreement: how much risk screening happens before surgery. Rhode Island and Kaiser put numbers on BMI (<40) and A1c (<8.1 vs <7.5); Blue Shield CA calls BMI >40 and age <50 only relative contraindications; Florida Blue has none. Conservative duration ranges from none specified (BSC) to 6 weeks of medication plus 3 PT visits (Kaiser) to 3 months (Florida Blue) to >12 weeks of three parallel therapies (Rhode Island).

Unique: Rhode Island tobacco abstinence, lab thresholds for infection (ESR >30, temperature >100.4 F), and ADL pain counts; Kaiser social exclusions (caregiver, home), unstable angina, rheumatologist requirement, medical-director review route; Blue Shield CA customized/gender-specific implant exclusion and allergy; Florida Blue resurfacing criteria (the others are silent).

Florida Blue vs others:
- Looser: no BMI, A1c, tobacco, age (for total hip), exam-finding or pain-quantity requirements; only one conservative modality is needed (even "weight reduction" or "assistive device use"); a single radiographic finding suffices; several diagnoses (fracture, malignancy, AVN, failed previous surgery) qualify by themselves.
- Stricter: fixed 3-month conservative requirement "clearly documented" (Kaiser 6 weeks plus 3 PT sessions; Blue Shield CA no duration); contraindications for neuropathic arthritis and rapidly progressive neurological disease (not in the others as written); resurfacing limited to age 55 or younger with a long contraindication list, while Rhode Island and Kaiser do not cover it by name.

## Structure

- Florida Blue: a MCG with Position Statement, starred contraindication footnote, and codes list; one flat OR list with the AND-list hanging off the last item.
- Rhode Island: heavily enumerated hierarchical criteria ("meeting all of the following (a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h)"), hip and knee interleaved, comorbidity block repeated in each pathway, lab-thresholded infection tree for revision.
- Blue Shield CA: general conditions A-G (mostly negations) then H list of indications; conservative treatment and relative contraindications are in Policy Guidelines; Appendix A side-by-side redline; long literature review.
- Kaiser: two-part ALL plus ONE-of structure, with nested ORs, soft exceptions (shared decision-making, medical director review), a separate Exclusions section with social factors, a coverage table by line of business.
- Delegation: Medicare to LCDs (L33618 for FL, L34163 for Kaiser); Rhode Island and Florida Blue cite InterQual only in references.

## Problems for an automated checker

- Florida Blue scoping: "Advanced joint disease ... AND o Pain or functional disability ... AND o Failure of at least 3 months ... No contraindications* to total hip arthroplasty for any indication above are present." Only the indentation shows the first two ANDs belong to one branch while the contraindication line applies to all.
- Florida Blue "Failure of previous hip surgery": undefined; "Clinically significant leg length inequality / audible noise / progressive or substantial bone loss": no measure.
- Florida Blue "Severely overweight (without attempted weight loss or discussion of increased risk related to BMI)": no BMI number.
- Rhode Island: "Needs to meet 1 or 2" with the conservative trial under branch 2 only; "AND ALL of the following for the above indications (1, 2, 3, 4)" attached by number; duplicate lettering ("c. Obesity" twice) and typos ("And The individual").
- Rhode Island: "C-reactive protein > normal", "Synovial WBC ... > normal" depend on the lab's reference range; "Prior authorization is recommended".
- Blue Shield CA: criterion A (reason must be OA/RA/AVN/post-traumatic arthritis) conflicts with H.2-H.5 (tumor, fracture, FAI, revision) in an ALL list; "AVN with greater than stage II with collapse and in stage II ..." (and = or; no staging system named); relative contraindications have no stated effect; conservative therapy "unless contraindicated" with no duration.
- Kaiser: "ALL the following are met: 1) ... 2) ... AND ONE of the following" with revision and fracture as options, so pain/ROM may or may not be required for them; "BMI < 40: if BMI is > 40" (BMI exactly 40?); "Dementia that interferes with successful rehabilitation", "unsuitable home situation" are not chart facts; ligatures ("ﬁndings", "inﬂammatory") break text matching; reference 4 (wild mammal study) shows reference-list errors; policy number appears as NCP 117 and "Policy 17.1".
- Chart-invisible: weight-loss attempts over months, tobacco-free interval, PT session counts, social support, "adequate efforts".

## Test cases

1. 62-year-old with advanced OA (bone-on-bone), BMI 43, HbA1c 8.6, current smoker, 4 months of NSAIDs, PT and activity changes. Florida Blue approves: imaging evidence, pain, 3 months conservative care, no listed contraindication, no BMI/A1c/tobacco rule. Rhode Island denies: "Obesity: BMI <40", "HgbA1C < 8.1%", "4 weeks off of tobacco use". Kaiser denies on automatic review (BMI >40 and A1c >7.5 require documentation, shared decision-making and medical-director review).
2. 58-year-old with radiographic joint space narrowing only and 3 months of weight reduction as the only treatment. Florida Blue approves: "1 or more" conservative measure including "Weight reduction as appropriate", and any single imaging finding. Blue Shield CA denies: needs two of six findings and the full conservative set unless contraindicated. Rhode Island denies: no bone-on-bone and fewer than 2 findings, plus NSAIDs >3 wk, PT/HEP >12 wk, activity modification >12 wk all required.
3. 66-year-old with moderate OA (osteophytes, bone-on-bone), BMI 31, A1c 6.5, 8 weeks of continuous acetaminophen plus NSAIDs and 3 PT sessions last year. Kaiser approves: 6 weeks of both drugs and at least 3 PT sessions. Florida Blue denies: "Failure of at least 3 months of conservative non-surgical management". Rhode Island denies: PT/HEP >12 weeks and activity modification >12 weeks not met.
