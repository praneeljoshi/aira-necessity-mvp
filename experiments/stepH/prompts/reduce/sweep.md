## Persist until the tree is exhaustive
Don't stop at the first tree that passes. Before you submit, read the whole policy once more from top to bottom, every
section including notes, definitions, coding/reimbursement, limitations and experimental/investigational lists. For
each sentence that could limit approval under this pathway, make sure it is in the tree, or give one line in notes on
why it doesn't apply. Then repeat the patient self-check: a patient who meets your logic but whom any sentence of the
policy would deny. Submit only when a full pass adds nothing.
